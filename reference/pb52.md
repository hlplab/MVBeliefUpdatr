# Peterson & Barney (1952) Vowel Acoustic Data

Acoustic measurements of 10 American English vowels produced by 76
speakers (men, women, and children) in /h-V-d/ words. This dataset is a
standardized copy of the dataset bundled in the phonTools package
(Barreda 2015) , originally published by Peterson and Barney (1952) .

## Usage

``` r
data(pb52)
```

## Format

A tibble with 1,520 rows and 9 variables:

- speaker:

  Speaker identifier as a factor.

- sex:

  Speaker sex as a factor with levels `"female"` and `"male"`.

- type:

  Speaker demographic group as a factor with levels `"men"`, `"women"`,
  and `"children"`.

- vowel:

  Standardized Unicode IPA vowel label in phonemic slashes as a factor
  with 10 levels (see `levels(pb52$vowel)`).

- repetition:

  Repetition index (1 or 2).

- f0:

  Fundamental frequency in Hertz (Hz).

- f1:

  First formant frequency in Hertz (Hz).

- f2:

  Second formant frequency in Hertz (Hz).

- f3:

  Third formant frequency in Hertz (Hz).

## Source

Standardized from `phonTools::pb52` (Barreda 2015) .

## References

Peterson GE, Barney HL (1952). “Control methods used in a study of the
