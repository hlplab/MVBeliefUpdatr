# Hillenbrand et al. (1995) Vowel Acoustic Data

Acoustic measurements of 12 American English vowels produced by men,
women, boys, and girls in /h-V-d/ syllables. This dataset is a
standardized copy of the dataset bundled in the phonTools package
(Barreda 2015) , originally published by Hillenbrand et al. (1995) .

## Usage

``` r
data(h95)
```

## Format

A tibble with 1,668 rows and 9 variables:

- speaker:

  Speaker identifier as a factor.

- sex:

  Speaker sex as a factor with levels `"female"` and `"male"`.

- type:

  Speaker demographic group as a factor with levels `"men"`, `"women"`,
  `"boys"`, and `"girls"`.

- vowel:

  Standardized Unicode IPA vowel label in phonemic slashes as a factor
  with 12 levels (see `levels(h95$vowel)`).

- f0:

  Fundamental frequency in Hertz (Hz).

- f1:

  First formant frequency in Hertz (Hz).

- f2:

  Second formant frequency in Hertz (Hz).

- f3:

  Third formant frequency in Hertz (Hz).

- duration:

  Vowel duration in milliseconds (ms).

## Source

Standardized from `phonTools::h95` (Barreda 2015) .

## References

Hillenbrand J, Getty LA, Clark MJ, Wheeler K (1995). “Acoustic
characteristics of American English vowels.” *The Journal of the
